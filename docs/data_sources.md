# Data sources

## CSO — Electricity Supply by Source

**Purpose:** annual and monthly grid-scale generation, electricity imports/exports and storage.

Official release:
https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-ess/electricitysupplybysource2025/

Relevant tables:
- ESSA02 — monthly electricity supply by source
- ESSA04 — annual electricity supply by source

## SEAI — Electricity statistics / generation

**Purpose:** long-run electricity generation by source and transformation context.

https://www.seai.ie/data-and-insights/seai-statistics/electricity

https://www.seai.ie/data-and-insights/seai-statistics/annual-energy-data/energy-transformations/electricity-generation

## SEAI — National Energy Balance

**Purpose:** public thermal power-plant fuel inputs, electricity outputs, indigenous production, imports and transformation accounting.

https://www.seai.ie/data-and-insights/seai-statistics/key-publications/national-energy-balance

## SEAI — Energy supply / security

**Purpose:** independent interpretation of indigenous versus imported energy and gas dependence.

https://www.seai.ie/data-and-insights/seai-statistics/key-publications/energy-supply-security

## SEAI — Conversion factors

**Purpose:** natural-gas combustion factor used in the gas carbon-cost sensitivity.

https://www.seai.ie/data-and-insights/seai-statistics/conversion-factors

## European Commission — EU ETS

**Purpose:** carbon-price benchmark for gas-generation sensitivity.

https://climate.ec.europa.eu/areas-action/carbon-markets/eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/auctioning-allowances_en

The European Commission reports an average 2025 EU ETS auction price of €73.43/tCO₂.

## Gas Networks Ireland — Imbalance prices

**Purpose:** Irish gas-market/SAP framework and current IBP/NBP SAP data.

https://www.gasnetworks.ie/about/data-transparency/balancing-actions-and-prices/imbalance-prices

## ONS / National Gas Transmission — System Average Price of gas

**Purpose:** official daily GB OCM SAP series, useful as wholesale gas-market context.

https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/economicoutputandproductivity/output/datasets/systemaveragepricesapofgas
