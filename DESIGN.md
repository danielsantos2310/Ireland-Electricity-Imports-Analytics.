# Design system

## Creative direction
Energy-system evidence story: deep navy canvas, electric green for renewable generation, warm amber for gas/cost exposure, cyan for imports/system infrastructure, and muted grey for methodological limitations.

## Palette
- Background: `#07131C`
- Surface: `#0D1E29`
- Surface 2: `#102936`
- Text: `#F4F8FA`
- Muted: `#9AB0BA`
- Renewable: `#7ED8A4`
- Gas / cost: `#F3B45D`
- Imports / system: `#69D8E7`
- Risk / constraint: `#F27668`
- Rule: `#233945`

## Interaction
No external charting dependency. Canvas charts redraw responsively. Core narrative remains readable without JavaScript.
